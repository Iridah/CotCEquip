## Passive Skills

<aside>
<img src="Relm/Paint.png" alt="Relm/Paint.png" width="40px" /> **Colourful Palette**
After taking action, if the user has been granted any kind of the special effects “Paint” of a specific colour combination,  grant All Allies the following “Paint” effects based on corresponding colours.
·“Paint: Red” + “Paint: Blue”: Grants the special effect “Paint: Purple” (turns: 3/granted as a battle skill).
·“Paint: Red” + “Paint: Yellow”: Grants the special effect “Paint: Orange” (turns: 3/granted as a battle skill).
·“Paint: Blue” + “Paint: Yellow”: Grants the special effect “Paint: Green” (turns: 3/granted as a battle skill).
