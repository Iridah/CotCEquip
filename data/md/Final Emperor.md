## Passive Skills

<aside>
<img src="Final%20Emperor/Emperor-Inspiration.jpg" alt="Final%20Emperor/Emperor-Inspiration.jpg" width="40px" /> **“Emperor’s Inspiration”**
Gain the ability to exploit Sword, Polearm, Dagger, Axe, Bow, Staff, Tome and Fan weakness with some physical attacks other than the “Attack” action (damage calculation based on original attack). Enhance Boost Levels to Boost Lv. 2/Lv. 4/Lv. 6 when consuming 1, 3 or 5 BP, respectively.
When consuming 3+ BP: Grant Self Dead Aim (guaranteed critical hits) and raise the critical damage multiplier (1.25→1.5).

</aside>

<aside>
<img src="Final%20Emperor/BP_Recovery_Boost.png" alt="Final%20Emperor/BP_Recovery_Boost.png" width="40px" /> **(1★)**

Def. of Entire Front Row by 20% and reduce their damage taken from some attacks by 10% (Does not stack with effects of the same type). Also grant Entire Front Row automatic HP recovery (potency: 50) and automatic SP recovery (amount: 2% of target’s max SP).

</aside>

·7+: Raise potency of Self by 50% (Does not stack with effects of the same type).
·8: Raise Damage Limit of Self by 100,000.
